## Calls for Lane (20)

1. **First/last (19:30, 20:8, 20:10, 20:16, 20:27).** (a) Local root for this stretch only, not a book-wide thread (recommended: prōtos is mostly ordinary "first", eschatos has a different sense at 12:45). (b) Promote book-wide as `first-last`.
2. **Jonah 4 echo at 20:12 "scorching heat".** (a) Ship as a tentative aside.echo (recommended: rare shared word kausōn plus the same situation, anger at God's generosity to outsiders; no commentator draws it). (b) Drop.
3. **Genesis 1:28 echo at 20:25 "lord it over" (katakyrieuō).** (a) Ship as tentative aside.echo (recommended: same rare verb; word-level only). (b) Drop.
4. **Story box: Bathsheba before David (1 Kings 1) beside the mother's request.** (a) Yes, tentative (recommended: D&A offer it as one of four explanations; proskyneō and kathizō both in the LXX). (b) No.
5. **Story box: David's equal share (1 Samuel 30) beside the parable.** (a) Yes (recommended: shared point of equal share by gift; D&A call it striking; no shared vocabulary, so say so in the box). (b) No.
6. **Rendering of diakonos (20:26, "waits on others").** (a) "servant" (recommended; diakoneō "serve", doulos stays "slave"). (b) "attendant".
7. **Rendering of hetairos (20:13, master's word to the grumbler).** (a) "friend" (recommended; D&A: civil, perhaps no name known). (b) "comrade".

## Decided without Lane
