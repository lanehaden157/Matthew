# Matthew Study — Reference Links

The full annotated list of outside sources for the research passes. The quick-lookup
URL patterns used in artifacts live in `matthew_study_style_reference.md` §5; this file
is the longer shelf behind them.

What's uploaded to the research project itself (Greek and English texts, Constable, the
Bible Project teacher notes), and how to read those files, is in `instructions.md` under
"Files". Short version: the three `.pdf` files are plain text, so read them with
`grep -a` / `sed`, and strip the NASB page markers.

## Greek text and lexical tools

- **SBLGNT**, free download: https://sblgnt.com/download/
- **Biblehub Interlinear, Matthew**: verse-by-verse Greek with parsing.
  https://biblehub.com/interlinear/matthew/1.htm (change the chapter number in the URL)
- **Logeion**: LSJ and Middle Liddell. The fastest free Greek dictionary.
  https://logeion.uchicago.edu/
- **Perseus Digital Library**: LSJ and classical texts. https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/

## English translations

- **Young's Literal Translation, Matthew by chapter**: https://ebible.org/engylt/MAT01.htm
  (MAT01 through MAT28)
- **Lexham English Bible**, a free hyper-literal translation: https://lexhampress.com/LEB
- **BibleGateway**, NASB and multi-translation lookup:
  https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+1&version=NASB

## Commentaries

- **Constable's Notes on Matthew**, full PDF (also uploaded as `matthew.pdf`):
  https://soniclight.com/tcon/notes/pdf/matthew.pdf
- **Constable's Notes, chapter by chapter on StudyLight**:
  https://www.studylight.org/commentaries/eng/dcc/matthew.html
- **Chrysostom, Homilies on Matthew** (New Advent, all 90):
  https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/2001.htm. Homily N is `2001NN.htm` (200101.htm is
  homily 1). The premier patristic Matthew commentary; Matthew is the most-cited gospel in
  the patristic period.

## Second Temple Jewish texts

- **Early Jewish Writings**, main index: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/. The best
  free aggregator: Psalms of Solomon, 1 Enoch, 2 Baruch, 4 Ezra, Jubilees, Wisdom of
  Solomon, Sirach, Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs, Letter of Aristeas, and more.
- **Psalms of Solomon**: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/psalmsofsolomon.html.
  Psalm 17 (a Davidic Messiah who cleanses Jerusalem) is essential context for Matthew's
  messianic expectations.
- **1 Enoch**: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/1enoch.html. The Parables of Enoch
  (chs. 37–71) are essential for Matthew's Son of Man usage.
- **Jubilees**: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/jubilees.html
- **Wisdom of Solomon**: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/wisdomofsolomon.html.
  Critical for Matthew's Wisdom Christology and the "come to me" sayings.
- **Sirach / Ben Sira**: https://www.earlyjewishwritings.com/sirach.html
- **R. H. Charles, *Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha* (1913)**, Internet Archive:
  https://archive.org/details/apocryphaandpseu02char. Old but comprehensive; 1 Enoch,
  2 Baruch, Psalms of Solomon, 4 Ezra and Jubilees in one place.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls**, Israel Antiquities Authority:
  https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive. Key texts for Matthew: 1QS
  (Community Rule), CD (Damascus Document), 4QFlorilegium, 4QMMT. The Habakkuk Pesher
  (1QpHab) shows the pesher method that Matthew's formula quotations parallel.

## Hebrew Bible / LXX

- **NETS**, New English Translation of the Septuagint, one PDF per book:
  https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/ (Isaiah:
  https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/23-isaiah-nets.pdf). Essential for the formula
  quotations, which often follow LXX text-forms rather than the MT.
- **BibleHub**, parallel OT texts: https://biblehub.com/. For any OT passage Matthew
  quotes, MT and English translations side by side.
- **Targuman**, Christian Brady's blog on Targum traditions: http://targuman.org/targum/.
  Matthew's OT text-form sometimes tracks the Targum rather than MT or LXX, especially in
  the formula quotations and the Servant Song citations.

## Synoptic comparison

- **Gospel Parallels**, side by side: https://www.gospelparallels.com/
- **Table of Gospel Parallels** (after Aland's *Synopsis*):
  https://www.bible-researcher.com/parallels.html. A pericope-by-pericope index of where
  each passage appears across the four gospels.
- **Q, reconstructed text**: https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/q.html. Useful for
  isolating Matthew's editorial shaping of material shared with Luke.

## Patristics and early reception

- **Early Christian Writings**, main index: https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/.
  Ignatius, the Didache, Justin Martyr, Irenaeus and others who lean heavily on Matthew.
- **Didache**: https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/didache.html. The earliest
  non-canonical document with heavy Matthew dependence: its Lord's Prayer, mission
  instructions and community rules all reflect Matthean material.
- **New Advent, Church Fathers index**: https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/. Use for
  Jerome's Matthew commentary and other early interpreters alongside Chrysostom.

## Literary-canonical frame (the counterweight to Constable)

- **Bible Project Classroom teacher notes** (uploaded): `rise-of-the-messiah-teacher-notes.pdf`
  covers Matthew 1–4 (genealogy, birth, baptism, testing); `messianic-torah-teacher-notes.pdf`
  covers Matthew 5–8 (the Sermon on the Mount and the opening miracle cycle). They read
  from a narrative-theological, Jewish-literary frame where Constable reads from a
  dispensationalist/evangelical one. Hold the two in active dialogue. **They stop at
  chapter 8**; past that, the frame comes from the resources below.
- **Bible Project, Matthew overview** (literary/structural):
  https://bibleproject.com/explore/video/matthew/
- **Bible Project, Matthew podcast series**:
  https://bibleproject.com/podcast/series/matthew-book-series/
- **NT Wright Online**, articles and lectures: https://www.ntwrightonline.org/. Kingdom
  theology and Jewish context; many free articles.
- **Dale Allison, *The New Moses: A Matthean Typology*** (Semantic Scholar entry, abstract
  and contents only):
  https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/THE-NEW-MOSES-A-Matthean-Typology-Allison/13bf55d37995f6cd71d0d3bf58edb38ace796717.
  The essential monograph on Moses typology through Matthew.
